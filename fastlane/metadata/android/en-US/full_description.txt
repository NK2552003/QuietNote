QuietNote is a 100% private, offline-first study suite and productivity sanctuary.

Features:
- Study Notes & Markdown: LaTeX math, syntax highlighting, and PDF export.
- Tasks & Habits: Priorities, subtasks, and streak counters.
- Zen Focus Timers: Pomodoro and floating edge pill timer.
- Spaced Repetition: Active recall flashcards and courses.
- Biometric App Lock: Fingerprint and device security.
- Zero telemetry, zero ads, and 100% local SQLite storage.
