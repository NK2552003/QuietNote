QuietNote is a modern, privacy-focused, offline-first personal productivity space designed for deep work, study, and daily organization.

All your data stays safely on your device. QuietNote has zero tracking, zero analytics, zero external telemetry, and works completely offline.

Key Features:

• Rich Notes & Markdown: Fast distraction-free writing, Markdown formatting, LaTeX math formulas, syntax-highlighted code blocks, tables, and tags.
• Task Lists & Subtasks: Organize daily todos, priorities, due dates, and reminders with effortless checkbox workflows.
• Habits & Daily Routines: Build lasting habits, track streaks, and structure morning/evening routines with audio cues.
• Visual Calendar & Agendas: Manage daily timelines, course schedules, exams, and milestones in one unified view.
• Flashcards & Spaced Repetition: Create flashcard decks for exam revision with active recall and self-assessment scoring.
• Zen Focus & Study Timers: Pomodoro, 50/10, Deep Work, and custom intervals with an optional floating edge pill timer over other apps.
• Customizable Navigation Dock: Personalize the floating bottom dock with Compact, Standard, or Spacious sizing and Left, Center, or Right hand alignment for effortless one-handed use.
• Biometric App Lock: Protect your notes and sensitive thoughts with native fingerprint, face unlock, or device screen lock.
• Daily Journal & Mood Tracking: Capture reflections, gratitude, and daily highlights with private, encrypted local storage.
• Local Backup & Export: Full JSON database export, Markdown document exports, and PDF generation with bundled typography.

QuietNote is free and open-source software (FOSS) released under the Apache 2.0 license.
