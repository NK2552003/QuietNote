## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-16

### Added
- **Privacy & Security**:
  - 100% offline-first architecture with zero trackers, analytics, or external telemetry.
  - Native Biometric App Lock supporting fingerprint, face recognition, and Android device lock (PIN/pattern).
  - Private local SQLite database powered by Drift with automated schema management.
- **Customizable Floating Navigation Dock**:
  - Three calibrated dock sizes: **Compact** (48dp), **Standard** (54dp), and **Spacious** (60dp).
  - Ergonomic hand positioning: **Left**, **Center**, and **Right** horizontal alignment for comfortable one-handed reach with either thumb.
  - Live interactive preview in Appearance Settings with animated tab indicators.
- **Zen Focus & Study Suite**:
  - Customizable focus timers with Pomodoro, 50/10, Deep Work, and Custom interval presets.
  - Ambient focus bells and audio cues.
  - Optional high-precision floating edge pill overlay across other applications.
- **Rich Notes & Markdown**:
  - Distraction-free writing with full CommonMark support.
  - LaTeX mathematical equations (`$...$` and `$$...$$`).
  - Code syntax highlighting for 150+ programming languages.
  - Interactive checklists, tags, search, and instant PDF/Markdown exports.
- **Tasks, Habits & Routines**:
  - Priority task lists with due dates and optional notifications.
  - Habit streak tracker with consistency statistics.
  - Structured morning, afternoon, and evening routines.
- **Calendar, Courses & Flashcards**:
  - Unified timeline agenda for deadlines, exams, and milestones.
  - Academic course syllabus and lesson management.
  - Spaced repetition flashcards with self-assessment recall ratings.
- **Daily Journal & Reflections**:
  - Private journal entries with mood logs and gratitude prompts.
- **Distribution & FOSS Compliance**:
  - Published under the Apache-2.0 open-source license.
  - Prepared official F-Droid package metadata (`io.github.nk2552003.quietnote`).
  - Fastlane Triple-T store metadata and assets.
